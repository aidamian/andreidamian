02 / Entrepreneurship

Lummetry.AI

I built Lummetry.AI around applied AI research, with products for video analytics, forecasting, and text analysis. The Lummetry brand launched in 2018, growing out of Knowledge Investment Group.

In September 2021, Lummetry.AI was acquired by GTS Global Intelligence, part of Global Technical Group and backed by Abris Capital Partners. The acquisition closed on 15 September; I continued my work at Lummetry through 2025.

From Lummetry to the Hyperfy group

Lummetry’s AI capabilities joined GTS’s security software business. The group announced its Hyperfy rebrand in December 2022 and launched MotionMask, a product for anonymising people and vehicles in video, in January 2023.
